Sales Dashboard: one place to see plan, pipeline and people

A sales dashboard for a manufacturing IT services business. Each view answers one question leadership asks in its weekly and monthly reviews. This walkthrough uses made-up companies, people and numbers.

My part: I designed the views, the metrics, the questions each view answers and the review workflows. A developer built the React front end and deployed it.
